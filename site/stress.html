Two questions about noise. Can a local rule hold a stored bit against independent errors, and does its lifetime grow with the lattice? And where, in the Domany–Kinzel plane, does activity survive at all? Every figure here is a recorded run with its seed beside it, and a run that reached its cap is drawn as ≥ cap — never as a lifetime.

Toom's rule (NEC majority)

A cell takes the majority of itself, its north and east neighbours, and then every cell is flipped with probability ε. The rule is asymmetric on purpose: the stored bit survives noise that erases it under the symmetric majority of the four neighbours.

Domany–Kinzel phase diagram

A site becomes active with probability p1 if exactly one neighbour was active and p2 if both were. Bond directed percolation is the line p2 = p1(2 − p1), critical at p1 = 0.644700185 — the point whose density decay is fitted below.
