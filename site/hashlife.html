HashLife

A level-k node covers 2^k × 2^k cells and caches the centre square advanced 2^(k-2) generations, so the pattern is computed once per distinct subtree. Zoom out and a pixel becomes a node, drawn as its population; zoom in and it becomes a cell again. The tree view reports nodes against nominal cells, which is what makes the compression legible.
