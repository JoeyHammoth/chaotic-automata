      How to read the map

      Each pixel is one of the 262,144 Life-like rules, coloured by the selected metric. Undecided rules — runs that hit the step cap without resolving — are hatched rather than coloured, so "we do not know yet" never looks like a result. The disagreement layer marks cells where independent indicators (damage, entropy, period) imply different classes.

      The noise overlay re-colours the map by whether structure survives at an error rate ε: a measured excess neighbour agreement, interpolated between four stored noise levels. Hatched cells are collapsed — uniform, or indistinguishable from a random field at that density — so “nothing survived” is readable without relying on colour.
